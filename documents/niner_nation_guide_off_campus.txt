Top Ten Charlotte Restaurants to Visit Before You Graduate

Dining halls and on-campus fast food restaurants can get a little boring after a while. Here is a list of 10 off-campus restaurants to visit during your time at UNC Charlotte. Some are just a few steps away from campus and some are a quick drive or light rail ride.

---

Boardwalk Billy's
A UNC Charlotte classic right on the water. Boardwalk Billy's offers a wide variety of food options like seafood, burgers, and barbecue. Good for satisfying all your roommate's needs. They have front and back outdoor seating. Can be more packed on weekends.
Price: $$

---

Cava
The Mediterranean Chipotle. A quick and fairly healthy option with a wide variety of toppings and sauces to include in your pita or grain bowl. Slightly more expensive but portions are quite big and perfect for next-day leftovers. Easy to grab and go, with curbside pickup available.
Price: $$

---

Midnight Diner
All UNC Charlotte students must go here before graduating. Recommend going late at night since it is open until 4:30am. Offers both breakfast and dinner options. Designed as an old-fashioned retro-style diner — the experience and atmosphere are just as appealing as the food. Can be more packed on weekends but totally worth the wait.
Price: $

---

Ninety's
Nothing better than dessert, especially ice cream. Located a few steps away from Boardwalk Billy's, this is the perfect after-dinner treat. Ninety's has been around for a while but recently revamped their inside with bright colors and 90s themed art featuring some local artists. Their signature Yum-E Buns and macaroni cream sandwiches offer a good rich dessert. Warning: your summer diet may hate this.
Price: $

---

Tequila's Taco and Bar
Located right across from campus, Tequila's is a great option for good drinks and authentic Mexican food. Replaced Macado's, another UNC Charlotte staple that was taken out by the pandemic.
Price: $$

---

Topper's Pizza
Located right off campus, Topper's is a good switch-up from your regular Pizza Hut, Domino's, or Papa John's. Known for its unique toppings. Convenient and great for college students.
Price: $

---

Zada Jane's
A two-story authentic Mexican restaurant with a nice view of the area. A small family-friendly restaurant that can be packed especially on weekends. Recommended during the week. Outdoor seating also available.
Price: $$

---

Haberdish
Another restaurant a little further from campus located in North Davidson (NoDa). One of the best fried chicken spots around. Known for southern food, great cocktails, and outdoor seating. Must warn you that they can be packed on weekends. Plan accordingly.
Price: $$

---

Lupie's Cafe
A neighborhood spot that once you find it, you won't forget it. Known for hibachi chicken. A good addition to your order takes it to the next level.
Price: $$

---

Note: If you or someone you know is experiencing hunger, visit the Jamil Niner Food Pantry at 1224 John Kirk Drive.
